Preeti to Nepali Unicode

Type with your normal Preeti keys. The Nepali appears right here as you type, and you can copy it straight into Facebook or Zoom chat.

Typing: नेपाली (Preeti)

Ctrl + Enter copies and clears, F2 switches between Nepali and English typing. Select all and press Backspace to clear. Typing and deleting always happen at the end of the text. Alt-code keys like ˆ, ¥ and ¿ work too.
